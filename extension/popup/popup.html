Why MEH MODE?

You open social media to do one thing. Then, somehow, twenty minutes disappear.

MEH MODE makes the sites you choose feel quieter without blocking them. Everything still works, but there is less color competing for your attention.

How it works

Choose your sites and browse normally in grayscale. Need color? Hold Peek in color. Release it and the page goes back to gray.

Turning a site's limits off asks you to write why. Your answer is not judged or saved. It simply gives you a moment to decide what you want to do.

We are still testing whether this helps people scroll less while getting their work done. It may not work the same way for everyone.

Stays on your device

Only your site choices, your daily limits and today's minutes used are saved. No accounts, browsing history, analytics, or data sent by MEH MODE. What you type in the entry questions is erased when you go in.

MEH MODE needs permission to change the appearance of the social sites you choose. It uses this access only to apply or remove grayscale. It does not read, save, or send your posts, messages, form entries, or other page content.

Turning limits off keeps website access, and they return at the next reset. Answers are never saved or sent. You can revoke access or uninstall through Chrome.
